These files contain derived patient-level statistical results used for numerical verification of selected findings from a study of patient-specific geometric calibration in stroke gait.

The original StrokePiG motion-capture dataset was published separately and is not redistributed in this repository.

Original dataset: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24192483.v1

Original publication: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02767-y

Users should provide appropriate attribution to the study authors and the original StrokePiG dataset publication when reusing these materials.

The CSV files use consistent pseudonymous patient identifiers. These identifiers do not guarantee complete anonymization.
